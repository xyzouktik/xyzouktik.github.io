Interdisciplinary builder across AI, computer vision, robotics and finance — co-founder of Underwater AI and iFiNN.

I am in my final year of an Integrated BS-MS at IISER Kolkata (Biological Sciences major; Computer Sciences minor, Humanities minor), with two papers under review and a master's thesis on detecting and identifying species from biological images and videos.

Selected Projects

Underwater AI

Co-founder & COO

Computational marine imagery: real-time restoration, detection and 3D reconstruction that run on the vehicle rather than topside. Built around prototype underwater-robotics hardware with in-house 3D printing, and trained on the Indian Ocean Dataset alongside EUVP and UIEB. Deployed on the ABYSS-1 ROV and backed by the MeitY Startup Hub, Government of India.

Founded January 2026 · India · Pre-seed stage · Backed by the Genesis EiR Cohort 2.0 grant · 1st place, pitching contest, Inter IISER–NISER Entrepreneurship Summit (IISER Tirupati)

underwaterai.org · github.com/Underwater-AI

iFiNN — Intelligent Financial Neural Network

Co-founder & CEO / Managing Partner

An AI-powered quantitative finance platform: assisted financial analysis, strategy development, backtesting and automated execution, with machine-learning workflows and a no-code surface for quantitative analysis. Finalist at the IIT Delhi Incubator Summit and the IIT Madras I-Summit.
